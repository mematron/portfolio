ARMINTA

Autonomous AI

An autonomous causal discovery agent that runs around the clock on its own machine. It watches the system, tries real actions, measures what changed, and keeps only the cause and effect links it can confirm.

  • Runs 24/7 as a self directed Python agent, no one at the keyboard
  • Samples 29 system metrics and picks its own mode: observe, investigate, optimize, dream, or self assess
  • Records confirmed causal edges and remembers every intervention

SUKOSHI

Browser AI

A little AI that lives, learns, and even dreams entirely inside your web browser. Give it a topic and it researches on its own, builds a personal knowledge base, and shows you how its ideas connect.

  • Rated 5.0 out of 5 on itch.io
  • Learns with Q-learning and evolves with genetic algorithms
  • Runs fully in the browser, no server, built with vanilla JavaScript
